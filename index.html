About the Town

A Town of Character & Charm

Kestel is a district and municipality of Bursa Province in northwestern Turkey, located 14 km east of Bursa city center on the road to İnegöl. With the forested slopes of Uludağ rising to the south and the broad Bursa plain stretching to the north, the town occupies one of the most naturally beautiful settings in the Marmara region.

Home to over 74,000 residents spread across 35 neighbourhoods, Kestel has grown steadily from a quiet frontier settlement into a thriving district of greater Bursa. It retains a strong community identity, a passion for local produce and food traditions, and an accessibility that makes it an easy and rewarding destination for visitors to the Bursa area.
