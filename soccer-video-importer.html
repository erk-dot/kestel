Soccer Video Importer

A personal desktop app for match video backups

What it is

Soccer Video Importer is a small personal desktop app for Linux. It copies soccer match videos from camera memory cards to a local backup drive, then uploads the videos and photos to a folder in the user's own Google Drive.

How it uses Google

The app signs in with Google only so it can see which match folders already exist in the user's chosen Drive folder, create new match folders there, and upload new video and photo files into them. It does not read, change, share or delete any other Drive content.

Who it is for

The app is for the developer's own family use. It is not sold or offered as a public service.
